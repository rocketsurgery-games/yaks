## Observations

- **The coordinator's merge queue was the bottleneck at the end.** Once the
  wave landed, lanes waited 25–80s for integration (each merge ran the full
  workspace test gate before committing). Later merges waited longer and conflicted more, because
  every lane had branched from a `main` that was falling further behind.
- **Lane size tracked scope, not yak count.** The fastest lanes (scroll 88s,
  openid 168s) were single-yak and local. The slowest (labels 505s, attach
  454s, paste 447s) crossed CLI+TUI+docs, or needed root-causing.
- **The scouts earned their keep.** At ~1–2 min each, they turned 4 fuzzy
  yaks into concrete decision questions and found one real bug.
- **Scoping by function kept textual conflicts cheap, but semantic conflicts
  appeared anyway.** They came from lanes that changed a *shared seam*: the
  event-loop dispatch, the nav-history entry type, and the form's row layout.
  The pre-flight shared-type scan checks struct literals, but it wouldn't catch
  "a lane replaces a function another lane extends", or "a lane adds a row that
  shifts another lane's test navigation".
