yaks log (yaks-c9ee) surfaces notes only; state changes (shave/shorn/regrow) are not individually timestamped, since status is implicit in the directory. To include transitions in the activity log, record a timestamped transition entry when move_task runs (an appended marker or a sidecar), then have herd::log merge notes + transitions. Keep it cheap and files-authoritative.
