When you see yaks-abcd, it's easy to identify the pattern that we then validate against yak ids, to decide whether or not to turn it into a link. But we should also be able to handle implicit prefixes of the form abcd, as long as yak-id validation is fast enough. We'd have to tokenize body text, isolating valid 4-character sequences that match the id-generation schema. The tokenization might have to be pretty lenient on treating things like whitespace, punctuation, etc as token delimiters.

But this would be really convenient, given that humans and agents _both_ have a habit of shorthanding yak ids without prefixes.

At least tangentially related to yaks-15f7, in that we'll have to deal with ambiguity in implicit prefixes.
