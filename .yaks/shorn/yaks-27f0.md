Establish + document (in yaks-working AND yaks-coordinating) the practice of running GENUINE design decisions -- even ones the agent has a clear lean on -- through the yaks ask/answer cycle, so the decision, the coordinator's rationale, and the human's call are captured as an attributed, durable thread on the yak. Motivation (Joel): builds long-term decision-making threads for future 'yak archaeology'. Nuance: this EXTENDS the existing 'ask when you cannot judge' guidance with 'ask to RECORD a judgment call even when you could make it', when the decision carries archaeological value. Guardrail: reserve for genuine forks (architecture, semantics, scope), not trivia -- record the lean in the ask so the thread shows reasoning, not just a verdict.
